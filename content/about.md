I'm a principal data scientist at a large retailer, working on machine learning, natural language processing, and AI.

Before that I was a Senior Data Scientist and Core Labs member at [Aware](https://www.awarehq.com/). Before that I was an assistant professor at the University of Missouri in [Journalism](https://journalism.missouri.edu/) and [Data Science](https://dsa.missouri.edu/), where I studied political communication and social media and wrote open-source R packages like [rtweet](https://github.com/ropensci/rtweet).

On my own time I build side projects with data. The most recent is [hoop2vec](https://mkearney.github.io/hoop2vec/), a basketball site with daily ratings and forecasts for men's and women's college basketball, the NBA, and the WNBA. The rest are on the [projects](/projects/) page, and I write about how they work [here](/post/).
