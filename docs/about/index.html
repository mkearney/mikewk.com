About

I’m a Principal Data Scientist at Meijer, working across data science, natural language processing, and AI.

Before Meijer I was a Senior Data Scientist and Core Labs member at Aware. Before that I was an assistant professor at the University of Missouri in Journalism and Data Science, where I studied political communication and social media and wrote open-source R packages like rtweet.

These days my side projects are AI agents that run on a schedule: pix, which publishes one data visualization a day, and daily, a morning news brief. I write about how they work here.
