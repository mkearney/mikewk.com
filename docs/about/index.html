About

I’m a principal data scientist at a large retailer, working on machine learning, natural language processing, and AI.

Before that I was a Senior Data Scientist and Core Labs member at Aware. Before that I was an assistant professor at the University of Missouri in Journalism and Data Science, where I studied political communication and social media and wrote open-source R packages like rtweet.

On my own time I build side projects with data. The most recent is hoop2vec, a basketball site with daily ratings and forecasts for men’s and women’s college basketball, the NBA, and the WNBA. The rest are on the projects page, and I write about how they work here.
